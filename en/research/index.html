Research

One Path,
Different Testing Grounds

We start from concrete technological and social problems, let technological arrangements expose the limits of existing concepts, and then rebuild freedom, agency, politics, and human imagery. Our different research topics test the same research program in different problem fields.

Technology is not treated only as an object to be evaluated. It is also a site from which inherited philosophical and political concepts can be tested and rebuilt.
