About

Starting from concrete problems,
rebuilding how we think about technology.

Technosophy Lab develops a problem-driven research program concerned with how technologies, environments, and institutions shape our behavior—and what those interactions require us to rethink about design, freedom, politics, and human agency.

Coinage

Technosophy社技哲學

Technosophy is coined in contrast to philo-sophy, the love of wisdom.

It asks not only how philosophy evaluates technology, but what happens when we start from technology to test and re-examine established philosophical concepts. Technologies and material arrangements shape how we act, decide, and live together, and in doing so they expose the limits of familiar beliefs about freedom, agency, politics, and human imagery.

The Chinese name, 社技哲學, gathers society (社), technology (技), and philosophy (哲學) into a single word. 社技 is also a homophone of 設計, design: society and technology constitute each other and cannot be understood apart, so in building and shaping the world we have to think about, and work on, both at once.

Problems before Disciplines

Disciplines are resources, not containers for thought. The order runs from problem, to the conceptual resources it needs, to the reconstruction of a framework.

Philosophy from Technology

Technology plays three roles in our research at once: it supplies empirical material, raises conceptual problems, and becomes the object of intervention after reconstruction.

After Diagnosis, Construction

Diagnosis matters, but the core work is building arrangements that can work, fail, be criticized, and be revised.

Major Work

Design for Green: Ethics and Politics for Behavior-Steering Technology is the fullest statement of this research program so far

Published in Chinese in 2026, the book asks whether, whether it should, and how technology may intervene in human behavior—and how this rewrites our understanding of freedom, human imagery, and democracy. Technosophy Lab was founded so that the book's questions, methods, and theoretical framework can continue to be developed, tested, criticized, and extended.
